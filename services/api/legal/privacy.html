Política de privacidad (Spanish summary available on request).

This Privacy Policy explains how C & M Gomez Enterprises LLC, doing business as Cocina Elvis ("we", "us"), handles information when you call our restaurant, place an order by phone or online, or receive text messages from our phone system ("Elvi").

Information we collect

  • Your phone number (caller ID) and, if provided, your name;
  • Order details (items, special instructions, pickup or delivery, payment status);
  • Call recordings and automated transcripts of calls to our restaurant phone line;
  • Delivery address, when you request delivery;
  • For staff members who opt in to SMS call alerts: their mobile phone number and opt-in record.

How we use it

  • To take, prepare, deliver and confirm your orders, and to recognize returning callers (for example, to offer a saved delivery address);
  • To send call and order alerts by SMS to our staff phones so calls are answered quickly;
  • To improve order accuracy and our phone ordering system, and for quality and training;
  • To process payments through our payment provider (Square) and communications through our phone provider (Twilio), which act as our service providers.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties or affiliates, except service providers (such as our messaging provider) strictly to deliver the messages.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for orders, records and the purposes above, and protect it with reasonable safeguards. Call recordings may be deleted periodically.

Your choices

You may ask us to access or delete your information by contacting us below. Staff can stop SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
